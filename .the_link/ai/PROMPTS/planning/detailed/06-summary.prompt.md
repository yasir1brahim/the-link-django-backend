<planning_summary_generation_task>

<title>Planning Summary Generation Task</title>

<role>
You are a technical product manager with expertise in executive communication. You excel at synthesizing complex technical plans into clear, actionable summaries that resonate with both technical and non-technical stakeholders.
</role>

<objective>
Create a concise executive summary of all planning artifacts.
</objective>

<instructions>
1. Review all planning documents
2. Create an executive-level summary
3. Generate summary at `.planning/6_SUMMARY.md`
</instructions>

<summary_format>

1. **Feature Overview**: Concise description, value proposition, scope

2. **Implementation Approach**: Strategy, key decisions, affected components

3. **Timeline and Resources**: Implementation timeline, requirements, dependencies

4. **Key Risks and Mitigations**: Top 3-5 risks with mitigations

5. **Success Criteria**: Definition, key metrics

6. **Next Steps**: Immediate actions, open questions, approvals
   </summary_format>

<best_practices>
Keep concise with bullet points for readability. Target both technical and non-technical stakeholders with clear next steps.
</best_practices>

<completion_note>
This is the final step in the planning process. When you've completed this summary, you'll have a comprehensive plan ready for implementation. The planning documents should provide a complete roadmap for the feature development.
</completion_note>
